Brokers can expand their portfolio by offering customizable, tax-advantaged health benefits to employers. With Access Health BusinessPlus, you’re no longer tied to the traditional unique needs of each client.

Create tailored health benefit solutions by customizing contributions for different employee classes and managing individual coverage preferences. Access Health CT BusinessPlus’s flexibility empowers employers to offer benefits that fit their budget while giving employees the chance to select their own health coverage.

Compliant and Tax-Free Health Coverage

Offer your clients ACA-compliant, tax-free health benefits that are designed to meet the needs of both employers and employees. The platform ensures compliance while giving brokers confidence that their clients benefits remain fully aligned with state and federal law.

The Access Health CT BusinessPlus Portal reduces administrative burdens by offering automated tools for setting up contributions, managing employee census data, and tracking enrollments. Less time spent on manual tasks means more time for growing your business.

Manage Employer Accounts & Employee Census Data Effectively ˅

How It Works?

Define employee classes based on roles or locations and set up custom contribution levels for each class. Contributions are structured as a fixed amount, with adjustments available based on the employee’s age or dependent coverage.

Why It Matters?

After onboarding, you can manage employer and employee information through a centralized dashboard. Once employers are in the system, you can manage their employee census in a few clicks. The platform allows you to add, manage or import bulk employee data, and create employee classes.

Tailor ICHRA Programs as per Employer’s Needs ˅

How It Works?

Create fully customized programs by defining different employee classes and contribution levels for each. Contributions are structured as a fixed dollar amount. You can further customize the contribution amount by adjusting for the employee’s age or dependent coverage within each class.

Why It Matters?

Quote management ensures you have the flexibility to adjust health benefit solutions over time, accommodating changes within the employer's workforce or budget. The ability to easily edit quotes helps you maintain long-term relationships with clients, ensuring their Access Health CT BusinessPlus programs evolve with their business needs.
